How to play

🎯

Collect the trash

Sweep bottles, cans, nets and tyres off the seabed. Chain pickups fast to build a score multiplier.

🫧

Watch your air

Dolphins breathe. Surface before the air meter empties — breaching at speed refills it instantly.

📡

Echolocate

Ping the dark. Sonar reveals buried trash, hidden caves and trapped animals through the gloom.

🐢

Free the trapped

Turtles in six-pack rings, seals in ghost nets. Swim into them to cut them loose — big bonus, and they'll follow you.

Touch

  • Drag anywhere on the left — steer
  • ⚡ button — boost (hold)
  • 📡 button — sonar ping
  • Boost straight up to breach the surface

Keyboard

  • WASD / Arrows — swim
  • Shift — boost  ·  Space — sonar
  • Mouse — steer toward cursor (hold)
  • P / Esc — pause  ·  M — mute
